D · Imaging branch (foundation model)

Done

H&E tissue tiles pulled directly from the GDC tile server (no multi-GB .svs download) for 40 TCGA TNBC slides, embedded with Phikon (Owkin) — an open, non-gated pathology foundation model pretrained on TCGA histology. (UNI/CONCH from the original plan are HuggingFace-gated and need manual license acceptance on an account this environment doesn't have; Phikon is the public equivalent, and fitting since our slides are TCGA slides too.)

Honest null result: with no subtype label for the imaging cohort, we asked whether unsupervised image-embedding clusters line up with basal-marker expression from matched RNA-seq. They don't, at this sample size (n=39, Mann-Whitney p=0.72, PC1 correlation r=-0.12, p=0.45) — reported as-is rather than reframed to look positive.

E · Interactive demo

Done

Streamlit app (app.py): pick a real (anonymized) METABRIC patient, see the fusion model's predicted probability of basal-like subtype next to the known ground-truth call, plus a live local SHAP explanation. A "what if" panel lets you drag the model's top genes and watch the prediction update in real time. Verified working end-to-end locally.

Not yet deployed to HuggingFace Spaces — needs an HF account/token this environment doesn't have. Runs identically once deployed there.
